## Eye Break
#
is designed to help improve your screen habits by reminding you to take short, healthy breaks while using your computer.

Every 20 minutes, a 20-second timer appears and reminds you to look at something about 20 feet away. This follows the 20-20-20 rule, which can help reduce digital eye strain and fatigue.

Every hour, Eye Break also reminds you to take a 5-minute break so you can stand up, move around, and step away from the screen.

The app is still in beta, and I would love to hear your feedback. Please let me know what could be improved, changed, or added to make Eye Break more useful.
